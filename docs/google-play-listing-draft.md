## App name

SideFlip: Project Ledger

## Short description

Track flip costs, expenses, photos, profit, ROI, and Trade-Up Goals.

## Full description

SideFlip helps resellers and hobbyist flippers understand what they actually put into every project—and what they earned when it sold.

Track the complete story of a flip in one place:

• Record the purchase price and project details
• Add repair costs, parts, fees, and other expenses
• Track time spent working on a project
• Attach before, after, and progress photos
• Record the final sale and calculate realized profit and ROI
• Organize active and sold projects
• Decode supported vehicle VINs to prefill vehicle details
• Build Trade-Up Goals and track progress from one project to the next

SideFlip is useful for cars, motorcycles, lawn equipment, watches, tools, furniture, boats, electronics, and other resale projects.

SideFlip is a record-keeping tool. It does not hold, transfer, or process money from item sales.

Your records remain tied to your account so you can sign in and continue tracking your projects across supported devices. Account deletion is available from Settings.
